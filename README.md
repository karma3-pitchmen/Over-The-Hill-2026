## 🕹️ About

over the hill is a stylized off-road exploration game set across vast wilderness, with vintage 4x4s inspired by the golden age of offroading. Drive through valleys, mountains, and shifting biomes, uncovering trails, resources, objectives, and hidden corners alone or with friends. This Windows build surfaced early: raw, unfinished, and not meant to be public yet.

For players who want slow-burn exploration, vintage vehicles, challenging trails, and a quietly chaotic co-op drive through the wilderness.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🚙 **Vintage 4x4s** — Drive iconic off-road vehicles inspired by the 1960s through 1980s.
- 🗺️ **Open Wilderness** — Explore expansive natural environments spanning valleys, mountains, and varied biomes.
- 🧭 **Trail Navigation** — Navigate challenging routes, discover new areas, and uncover hidden locations.
- ☄️ **Meteorite Hunts** — Chase falling meteorites and gather valuable resources across the wilderness.
- 🔧 **Unlockable Progression** — Complete objectives to unlock vehicles, upgrades, customizations, and cosmetics.
- 👥 **Solo or Co-op** — Take the journey alone or explore with up to three other players.
- 🌲 **Chill Off-Roading** — A relaxed driving experience focused on exploration, scenery, and the road ahead.
